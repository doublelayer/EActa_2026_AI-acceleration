# Scientific provenance boundary

The corrected reference supplies regression settings. The five ASE databases in the parent project root are the source of truth and their SHA-256 fingerprints are recorded in `article/input_manifest.json`; fingerprint changes produce an alert for review. Current plotting inputs are the supplied prediction, role, descriptor and split files under `source_data/figure4/`; their claims and generated figures are checked separately. Historical model bundles have different hyperparameters and are excluded rather than passed off as the current model. A new model fit and parameter replay are not required by this manuscript-generation task. Any claim of actual replay needs the matching current coefficients. The comparison reference is excluded from generation.
