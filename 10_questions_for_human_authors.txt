Questions for the human authors, revised 12 October 2026

Recovered author decisions from the earlier project discussion: keep the scope to ORR; make broader predictions the next ML objective; skip the previously proposed item 4; item 5 remains undecided. The current fixed DFT set and planned 1176-catalyst screening are incorporated below. These recorded decisions should not be asked again.

1. What is the central new electrochemical insight from the final DFT set that an Electrochimica Acta reader should remember?
   Controls the principal Results claim, Abstract, and Conclusions.

2. What precise part of catalyst discovery has AI accelerated in this study: analysis, candidate prioritization, article composition, or an actually completed new search?
   Controls the title claim and prevents planned screening from being reported as completed discovery.

3. After the coauthor screens 1176 bare catalysts, what finding would genuinely strengthen or change the article's central conclusion?
   Controls whether the later screening is a core result or a bounded extension.

4. Which structural or electronic feature do you expect to transfer from the A2/B1–B2 DFT training set to the wider library with other dialdehydes, and what outcome would show that it does not transfer?
   Controls the extrapolation claim and applicability-domain discussion.

5. What scientific decision will you make from the predicted ranking: select structures for new DFT calculations, select molecules for synthesis, or test a mechanistic hypothesis?
   Controls the interpretation of future predictions and the next-step statement.

6. What should be the main contrast with Ifargan et al.: human-chosen electrochemical questions, source-to-figure traceability, or the ability to regenerate a complete paper from reusable data?
   Controls the Introduction's novelty claim and the AI-method discussion.

7. Which documented human intervention best demonstrates that the researchers corrected an important scientific error or redirected an unhelpful AI analysis?
   Controls the evidence for human oversight rather than a generic assurance.

8. What work do the human authors claim as their intellectual contribution, and what was delegated to the LLM?
   Controls Methods, CRediT, the AI declaration, and the Conclusions.

9. What reusable result would you want an independent researcher to reproduce first from the released ASE and JSON data?
   Controls the FAIR-data claim and the reanalysis discussion.

10. Which result from the planned 1176-catalyst screening would you consider strong enough to change the paper's interpretation of AI assisted catalyst discovery?
    Controls whether the screening extends the current narrative or changes its main scientific conclusion.
